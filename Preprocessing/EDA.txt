Eploratory Data Analysis

its process of analysing , visualising and understanding the data before you build any Ml model


# EDA Steps:

1. Viewing Data (head(),tail(),shape,info())
2. Summary Stats(mean,median,mode,std,max,quartiles)
3. Value counts (how many unique value ( great for categorical data))
4. Missing Value Analysis(where are the gaps , percentage of missing data)
5. Visualisation
6. Target Variable Exploration (how does your output relate to other variables)
